{# Lens used by the refraction rule in booking.css. Grey (128,128) is "no displacement"; R drives the horizontal sample offset and G the vertical. The shape of the ramp matters more than its strength. backdrop-filter clips the backdrop to the card's own box, so any displacement at the very rim samples from outside that box — where there is nothing — and leaves a hard seam a few pixels inside the border. So the lens is neutral at the rim, peaks in a band set in from it, and is neutral again in the middle: the bending happens where there is still backdrop on both sides to bend. The peak offset (~4px) stays well under that band's inset (~10px), which is what keeps the seam from reappearing. The rects are deliberately square-cornered. With a rounded `rx` the area outside the corner radius is transparent black — R=0, G=0 — which reads as maximum negative displacement and tore each corner. #}
